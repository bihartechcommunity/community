# Bihar Tech Community

🚀 Welcome to Bihar Tech Community (BTC).

**Bihar Tech Community (BTC)** is a community dedicated to empowering technology enthusiasts, professionals, startups, innovators, and aspiring builders from Bihar and beyond.

We believe talent is everywhere, but opportunities are not.

Our mission is to bridge that gap by bringing together students, developers, engineers, designers, founders, researchers, mentors, companies, and technology leaders to learn, collaborate, innovate, and grow together.

Whether you're a student starting your tech journey, a developer building products, a founder launching a startup, or a professional looking to connect with like-minded people, BTC is your community.

What you can expect:
• Networking opportunities
• Knowledge sharing sessions
• Startup and innovation discussions
• Career guidance and mentorship
• Tech events and community initiatives
• Collaboration with industry leaders

Together, let's build a stronger technology ecosystem for Bihar and create opportunities for the next generation of innovators.
